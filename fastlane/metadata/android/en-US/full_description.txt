A complete keyboard with voice dictation that delivers polished, ready-to-send text.

• Hold the space bar, speak, release: the text arrives already polished.
• On-device AI removes filler words, repetitions and spoken self-corrections, and fixes grammar and punctuation.
• Dictate verification codes and spelled-out passwords safely.
• AI writing tools: more formal, shorter, bullet points, e-mail, translation and spoken commands.

Privacy: Whisper and the language model run on your phone. Nothing you say or type is sent anywhere.
No ads, no accounts, no telemetry. Open source (Apache 2.0). Optimised for Brazilian Portuguese.
